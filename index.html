What is Crynova?

An open-source peer-to-peer digital currency, inspired by community values and favoured by friendly innovators worldwide.

At its heart, Crynova is a fun and friendly crypto movement that brings people together! It is also an open-source peer-to-peer cryptocurrency that utilises blockchain technology, a secure system that records transactions on a public ledger maintained by a network of computers. More than this, though, is an amazing, vibrant community of friendly folks just like you!
